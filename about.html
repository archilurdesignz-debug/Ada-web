Archilurdesignz and Architecture (ADA) is a forward-thinking studio serving clients across Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, bridging the gap between creative imagination and buildable reality. At ADA, we deliver more than visual appeal. Our architecture is practical, compliant, and responsive, ensuring every project stands structurally, functions effectively, and endures over time.

HOW WE WORK

Our Process

01

Initial Consultation

  • What you provide: Your design brief, site survey plan, and reference photos of styles you love.
  • Site Visit: We visit your project location (client covers transport logistics and the site visitation fee).
02

Proposal & Terms

  • Invoice & Contract: Within 24 hours of your consultation, we issue your project cost invoice and contract terms.
  • Deposit: Work begins once a 60%–70% initial deposit is confirmed (with the remaining balance due upon final delivery).
  • Receipts: Official receipts are issued immediately for every transaction.
03

Online Client Portal Access

Once payment is cleared, you receive a private dashboard link to track your project in real time, including:

  • Progress Tracking: Live overall completion percentages, project timeline countdowns, and execution stages.
  • Automatic Alerts: Real-time email or WhatsApp notifications whenever a new drawing or document is uploaded.
  • Project History: Clear records of active revision counts and contract terms.
  • Post-Delivery Review: A dedicated feedback tool to rate our service upon project completion.
04

Site Analysis & Conceptual Design

  • We perform a site analysis to optimize orientation, natural lighting, and airflow.
  • We draft initial floor plan schemes and conceptual 3D sketches for your feedback. (Initial concept tweaks at this stage do not count against your revision limit.)
05

Design Development & Team Coordination

  • Once you approve the concepts, we refine detailed drawings and coordinate with structural and engineering consultants.
  • You receive regular progress updates on your portal to review and flag any observations.
  • Revision Allowance: Includes up to 3 free revisions during this phase.
06

Final Delivery

  • We issue the complete, final architectural package (available in digital soft copy or printed hard copy).
  • The remaining project balance is settled upon final delivery.
THE PHILOSOPHY

OUR APPROACH

Architectural Design Services From Concept to Construction

At ADA, we provide professional architectural design services that transform ideas into clear, buildable solutions. Every project starts with understanding client needs, goals, and site conditions.

We translate concepts into functional architectural designs that respond to real-life use and environmental context. Our focus is always on clarity, usability, and performance.

We develop detailed construction drawings and coordinated documentation that integrate architectural, structural, and MEP systems. This ensures smooth project execution.

Our structured process improves efficiency, reduces design errors, and ensures seamless transition from concept design to construction delivery.

DESIGN INTELLIGENCE

Purpose-Driven Architectural Planning Solutions

Our architectural planning services are guided by purpose, functionality, and long-term usability. We design spaces that are practical, efficient, and visually refined.

We consider key design factors such as circulation, lighting, ventilation, and spatial flow. This ensures optimal performance of every space we create.

Our approach balances form, function, and user experience, resulting in well-structured and efficient architectural layouts.

The outcome is a high-performance design solution that responds to user needs, site conditions, and modern building standards.

DELIVERY & COMMUNICATION

Construction Drawings and Design Execution Support

We provide detailed construction drawings and architectural documentation that clearly communicate design intent for accurate site execution.

Our process includes 3D visualizations and architectural rendering services, helping clients and contractors understand the final outcome before construction begins.

This improves coordination between consultants, contractors, and clients, reducing errors and misinterpretation during construction.

By combining technical precision with clear communication, we ensure that every design is executed exactly as intended on site.
